# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds a welcoming environment includes being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, accepting responsibility for mistakes and apologising to those affected, and focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

Behaviour that is unacceptable includes sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks, trolling or insulting comments, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone is representing the project in public space.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private issue, or by contacting the maintainer directly through the details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, reviewed promptly and fairly, and the maintainer will respect the privacy and security of whoever reports.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.
